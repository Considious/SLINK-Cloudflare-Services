## frame-chocolate-v2

Use case: product-mockup. Create one production-ready SQUARE nine-slice UI border asset on genuinely TRANSPARENT background. Candy Cartel luxury dark chocolate and restrained gold foil. Flat front orthographic camera, no perspective, no cast background shadow. Frame touches canvas edges with a tiny transparent gutter. All artwork confined to OUTER 14% of canvas; inner 72% completely transparent, no fill. Straight uniform middle edge sections, rounded corner details contained in corner squares for CSS nine-slicing. Quiet and elegant, not fussy, minimal ornament, not jagged sugar crystals. NO letters, icons, skulls, objects in the middle, rope, chains, candy canes, pink, silver or Christmas. Make a slim luxurious molded DARK CHOCOLATE BAR frame, warm rich brown and subtle glossy bevels. Broad smooth continuous chocolate edges with very sparse evenly spaced chocolate segment seams, NOT lots of repeated tiny squares. Corners reveal a hint of pistachio filling under small peeled-back GOLD FOIL tips. One thin gold inner lip. The chocolate is clearly the main material, gold a restrained accent. No liquid drips protruding into center.
